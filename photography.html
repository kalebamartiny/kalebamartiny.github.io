Photography

In engineering, work often moves quickly: an assignment to complete, a deadline to meet, or a launch to achieve. There are expectations to manage, standards to meet, and a personal reputation to uphold.

I've found photography to be an escape from this. It makes me slow down and pay closer attention to my surroundings. Photography lets me capture a moment in time in a way that is unique to me.

None of these shots are perfect, but each one is mine and has its own story. Hover over a photograph for the camera settings, or open it for a closer look.
