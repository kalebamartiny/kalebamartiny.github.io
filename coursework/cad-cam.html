Course Objectives

Core objectives drawn from the course syllabus.

  • Apply design-for-manufacturability and assembly principles.
  • Create 2D drawings and 3D parametric models using CAD tools.
  • Use GD&T, features, constraints, mates, and configurations in part and assembly design.
  • Analyze and improve designs with simulation studies and material considerations.
  • Generate, simulate, and interpret CAM toolpaths, NC code, and G-code for CNC machining.

Course Overview

This course covered part modeling, engineering drawings, GD&T, analysis, and machining preparation within a production-oriented workflow.

My bracket study includes modeling, FEA, fixturing, and machining operations; my arbor-press study adds design revisions, material comparisons, assembly work, and Mastercam verification.

What I Did / Learned

  • I developed designs with manufacturability in mind rather than treating geometry as the final deliverable.
  • I connected analysis, documentation, fixturing, and CAM planning within a production-oriented workflow.
