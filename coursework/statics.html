Course Objectives

Core objectives drawn from the course syllabus.

  • Model and analyze rigid-body equilibrium systems using engineering mechanics.
  • Create complete 2D and 3D free-body diagrams.
  • Resolve forces, moments, reactions, and distributed loads in structural systems.
  • Analyze trusses, frames, machines, friction, centroids, and moments of inertia.
  • Use analytical, computational, and empirical methods to solve statics problems and evaluate assumptions.

Course Overview

This course established the mechanics foundation I use for equilibrium, free-body diagrams, forces and moments, structures, centroids, moments of inertia, friction, and beam loading.

The page is intentionally focused on the course itself because no separate project report was included in the supplied materials.

What I Did / Learned

  • I built the mechanics framework that supports my later work in structures, dynamics, and design.
  • I developed a disciplined approach to defining forces, constraints, and assumptions before solving a problem.
