# Aerospace Laboratory 1

**Course Focus:** Foundational aerospace laboratory methods connecting engineering theory to measured results.

## Topics Covered
- Introductory instrumentation and measurement systems
- Basic aerodynamic and structural experimentation
- Data reduction and plotting workflows
- Team-based lab execution and documentation

## Deliverables
- Weekly/biweekly lab reports
- Measurement and analysis worksheets
- Comparison of experimental data with analytical predictions
