# Innovative Design in Engineering

**Course Focus:** Human-centered and iterative engineering design process from problem framing to prototype-ready solutions.

## Topics Covered
- Problem definition and user/needs analysis
- Concept generation and decision matrices
- Rapid prototyping and design iteration
- Communication of engineering concepts to stakeholders

## Deliverables
- Concept portfolio and design rationale
- Team design reviews and presentations
- Final design package with implementation plan
