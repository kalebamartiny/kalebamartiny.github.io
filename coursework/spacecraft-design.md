# Spacecraft Design

**Course Focus:** End-to-end spacecraft conceptual design emphasizing mission definition, subsystem integration, and design tradeoffs.

## Topics Covered
- Mission objectives, requirements flowdown, and constraints
- Orbit selection and basic trajectory considerations
- Spacecraft subsystem sizing (power, thermal, ADCS, communications, structures)
- Mass, power, and reliability budgeting across the full vehicle
- Design reviews, risk assessment, and verification planning

## Deliverables
- Mission concept and system requirements package
- Subsystem trade study with selected architecture
- Integrated preliminary spacecraft design report
