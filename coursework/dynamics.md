# Dynamics

**Course Focus:** Motion of particles and rigid bodies using Newtonian, energy, and momentum methods.

## Topics Covered
- Kinematics of particles and rigid bodies
- Kinetics using force-acceleration methods
- Work-energy and impulse-momentum principles
- Planar rigid body dynamics applications

## Deliverables
- Problem sets covering analytical solution methods
- Exam-based demonstrations of dynamics modeling
- Applied engineering calculations for moving systems
