Laboratory work in sensing, actuation, system identification, and PID control of a quadcopter arm.

  • MMAE 415
  • Spring 2026
  • Sensors, actuation, and PID control
Course Objectives

Core objectives drawn from the course syllabus.

  • Acquire and interpret dynamic signals using Simulink and dSPACE.
  • Use accelerometers, gyroscopes, and digital sensors to measure system behavior.
  • Characterize actuators and command motors and servos with PWM signals.
  • Perform system identification and assess open-loop response.
  • Design and evaluate closed-loop PID control for a quadcopter arm.

Course Overview

This laboratory course paired Simulink and dSPACE with accelerometers, gyroscopes, PWM actuation, system identification, and closed-loop PID control.

My reports document the progression from duty-cycle calibration and open-loop angle control to proportional, PI, and PID control of a quadcopter arm.

What I Did / Learned

  • I worked directly with measured signals, calibration, system identification, and feedback tuning on a physical test article.
  • I compared open-loop behavior with proportional, PI, and PID closed-loop response.
