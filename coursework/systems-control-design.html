Systems Control & Design

Dynamic modeling, stability analysis, and feedback control for an aircraft pitch-control application.

  • MMAE 443
  • Fall 2025
  • Feedback control
Course Objectives

Core objectives drawn from the course syllabus.

  • Develop mathematical models of dynamic systems.
  • Use Laplace transforms to form transfer functions and calculate time and frequency response.
  • Explain stability and the purpose of closed-loop feedback.
  • Apply classical control-design methods.
  • Design feedback controllers to meet specified performance criteria.

Course Overview

This course focused on dynamic-system modeling, transfer functions, time and frequency response, stability, and classical feedback-control design.

For the final project, I applied those methods to aircraft pitch control, comparing a Boeing 747-100 with the MMAE 414 crop-duster model.

What I Did / Learned

  • I applied classical control methods to an aircraft pitch-control problem.
  • I translated mathematical models into time response, stability, and controller-performance conclusions.
