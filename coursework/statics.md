# Statics

**Course Focus:** Equilibrium analysis of particles and rigid bodies under static loading.

## Topics Covered
- Free-body diagrams and equilibrium equations
- Trusses, frames, and machines
- Internal forces in beams and structural members
- Friction and distributed loading problems

## Deliverables
- Structured equilibrium problem solutions
- Beam/truss analysis assignments
- Foundational calculations supporting later structures courses
