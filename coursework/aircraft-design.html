Course Objectives

Core objectives drawn from the course syllabus.

  • Apply engineering fundamentals to conceptual aircraft design.
  • Define requirements, initial size, and configuration layout for an aircraft concept.
  • Use trade studies and applicable FAA standards to guide design decisions.
  • Predict aircraft performance, stability, and control with available analysis methods.
  • Evaluate aerodynamic performance through flight simulation and communicate the resulting design.

Course Overview

This course provided a structured process for conceptual aircraft design, from requirements and initial sizing through geometry definition, trade studies, stability, control, and performance.

I applied these methods to Sky Combine using XFLR5 and MATLAB/Simulink across multiple flight conditions.

What I Did / Learned

  • I applied the course process to a complete aircraft concept, carrying requirements through sizing, configuration, and performance work.
  • I organized sizing, stability, and trade-study results into a coherent final design narrative.
