Spacecraft Design

Systems engineering, mission planning, and subsystem design for a requirements-driven spacecraft concept.

  • MMAE 412
  • Spring 2026
  • Systems engineering + mission design
Course Objectives

Core objectives drawn from the course syllabus.

  • Integrate prior engineering knowledge into spacecraft design work.
  • Define mission objectives, requirements, and constraints at the system level.
  • Develop orbit, launch-vehicle, propulsion, sizing, mass, and power decisions.
  • Analyze payload and spacecraft subsystems including power, communications, ADCS, and thermal control.
  • Communicate technical work clearly in a team design environment.

Course Overview

This course introduced me to spacecraft design as a systems-engineering process, connecting mission objectives, orbit selection, launch considerations, and subsystem integration.

I worked across propulsion, structures, power, thermal management, telecommunications, and ADCS while tying each decision back to mission requirements and constraints.

What I Did / Learned

  • I connected mission requirements to subsystem-level decisions across communications, power, data handling, ADCS, and thermal design.
  • I documented decisions and tradeoffs in a format appropriate for a formal design review.
