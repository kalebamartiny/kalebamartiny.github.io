Dynamics

Kinematics, kinetics, and dynamic-system design applied to a crossbow-style softball launcher.

  • ENGR 212
  • Spring 2025
  • Kinematics and kinetics
Course Objectives

Core objectives drawn from the course syllabus.

  • Model and analyze dynamic systems using mechanics, mathematics, and physical principles.
  • Calculate position, velocity, acceleration, force, work, and energy for mechanical systems.
  • Create complete free-body diagrams and analyze rigid-body motion.
  • Use analytical, computational, and empirical methods to solve dynamics problems.
  • Design, model, build, and evaluate a dynamic mechanical system against requirements.

Course Overview

This course covered particle and rigid-body motion, kinetics, energy and momentum methods, and dynamic-system modeling.

For the final project, I applied those methods to a crossbow-style softball launcher, refining its launch angle, velocity, loading, and controllability.

What I Did / Learned

  • I applied motion-analysis methods in a design-and-build setting where performance depended on controllable launch conditions.
  • I connected equations, mechanism choices, and observed behavior to refine the design.
