Course Objectives

Core objectives drawn from the course syllabus.

  • Build and evaluate basic analog and digital measurement circuits.
  • Acquire, visualize, and post-process experimental data with MATLAB and laboratory instruments.
  • Measure flow rate, pressure, velocity, and airspeed with standard aerodynamic instrumentation.
  • Interpret aerodynamic data for jets, airfoils, and supersonic-flow experiments.
  • Determine convective heat-transfer behavior from experimental measurements.

Course Overview

This laboratory course focused on instrumentation, data acquisition, and aerodynamic testing across multiple experimental setups.

My reports cover axisymmetric-jet analysis using pitot-tube data, NACA 0012 pressure-distribution analysis, and convective heat transfer from a heated cylinder in cross-flow.

What I Did / Learned

  • I collected, processed, and interpreted aerodynamic data across multiple experimental setups.
  • I converted raw measurements into engineering conclusions supported by plots, calculations, and uncertainty-aware interpretation.
