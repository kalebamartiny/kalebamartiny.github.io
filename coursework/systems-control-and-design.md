# Systems Control and Design

**Course Focus:** Modeling dynamic systems and designing feedback controllers to satisfy performance and stability requirements.

## Topics Covered
- Transfer functions and state-space modeling
- Time-domain and frequency-domain response metrics
- Controller design (PID, lead/lag, state-feedback)
- Stability analysis and robustness considerations

## Deliverables
- Simulation-based control design studies
- Controller tuning and response comparisons
- Technical memo on design choices and trade-offs
