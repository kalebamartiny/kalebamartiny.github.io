Innovative Design in Engineering

Human-centered design, user research, and iterative prototyping for a stair-transport concept.

  • ENGR 204
  • Fall 2023
  • Human-centered design
Course Objectives

Core objectives drawn from the course syllabus.

  • Apply a human-centered design process from empathy through testing.
  • Gather user needs and translate them into requirements and specifications.
  • Verify design decisions against defined requirements.
  • Use prototypes at appropriate levels of fidelity to demonstrate and evaluate a concept.
  • Document and communicate technical work effectively within a collaborative design team.

Course Overview

This course used user interviews, need-finding, concept generation, and iterative prototyping to guide the design process.

After interviewing elderly users, my team developed a box-shuttle concept for moving objects on stairs using a track, cleat, adjustor, and pulley system.

What I Did / Learned

  • I worked with my team to prioritize user needs before settling on a design direction.
  • I used interviews, requirements, and prototype feedback to guide technical decisions.
