# Aircraft Design

**Course Focus:** Conceptual and preliminary aircraft design using mission requirements, performance constraints, and trade studies.

## Topics Covered
- Mission profile definition and sizing requirements
- Wing loading and thrust-to-weight matching
- Constraint analysis and design point selection
- Weight estimation and iterative design loops
- Basic stability and control considerations in early design

## Deliverables
- Concept aircraft sizing workbook
- Constraint diagram and design trade study
- Preliminary configuration recommendation
