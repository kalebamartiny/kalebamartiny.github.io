# Aerospace Laboratory 2

**Course Focus:** Advanced aerospace experiments with emphasis on test planning, instrumentation, and data-driven analysis.

## Topics Covered
- Experimental setup and calibration procedures
- Aerodynamic/flight-related data acquisition methods
- Uncertainty estimation and error propagation
- Technical reporting and presentation of lab findings

## Deliverables
- Formal lab reports with processed data and plots
- Experimental methodology summaries
- Interpretation of results versus theory
