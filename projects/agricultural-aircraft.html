Project

Sky Combine Agricultural Aircraft

Team-led agricultural aircraft concept based on AIAA SHOTS design and ferry mission requirements.

  • MMAE 414
  • Aircraft Design I
  • Team lead

Sky Combine was a course aircraft-design project based on AIAA's 2024-2025 Safe High Operational Tempo Sprayer (SHOTS) agricultural-aircraft RFP; the report was not submitted for competition judging.

The team used the RFP's application and ferry missions to frame the aircraft concept, aerodynamic modeling, and performance evaluation.

As team lead, I coordinated the design effort and completed the glide-performance and level-flight/climb analyses used to assess the aircraft against those mission conditions.

2024-2025 AIAA SHOTS Requirements

Course project informed by the AIAA Safe High Operational Tempo Sprayer agricultural-aircraft RFP; not submitted for judging.

  • Design missionApply 2 gal/acre across a 400-acre field from a 50-nmi operational radius, with a 15-minute reserve.
  • Ferry missionAccommodate a human pilot for a 600-nmi ferry flight with a 15-minute reserve.
  • Performance limitsRemain below 19,000 lb gross weight and 250 kt, evaluated at ISA +30 F conditions.
  • Field performanceMeet a 1,000-ft ground run and 1,500-ft distance over a 50-ft obstacle from hard-packed dirt or grass.
